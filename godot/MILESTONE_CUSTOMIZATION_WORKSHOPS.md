# Customization Workshops

The medieval valley now supports a player-authored visual identity rather than decoration being limited to merchant curios.

## Workshop loop

After the `earn_coin` campaign step, the crafting panel offers plans for the Sculptor's Workshop and Garden Nursery. Both require a villager to operate. Open a completed workshop with `Space`, choose one of its eight catalog recipes, press the listed ingredient buttons to load materials, and collect the finished decoration from its output button. Selecting the decoration in the hotbar immediately enters normal ghost placement mode.

The selected catalog recipe, loaded ingredients, production progress, output inventory, durability, and building worker all use the existing save system. A recipe cannot change while its batch is running.

Every piece has its own world sprite, footprint, inventory icon, ghost preview, placement collision, and beauty value. Small garden pieces award 2 Beauty, major statues award 3, and landmark decorations award 4.

The four First Living Year buildings and the two workshops share one polished medieval building atlas derived from the established windmill, granary, and forge art direction. The sixteen ornaments use a companion atlas with the same perspective and lighting.
