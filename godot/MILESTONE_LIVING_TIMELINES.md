# Living Timelines: Medieval Valley

This vertical slice tests the `one more day` structure before it is generalized
to every era.

## Daily loop

- A twelve-minute day visibly moves through dawn, daylight, sunset, and night.
- Weather and tomorrow's forecast appear in the HUD and Valley Journal.
- Manual gathering, crafting, and placement spend player energy.
- Select a Bread loaf and press Space away from a target to restore 25 energy.
- Press `N` to sleep, restore energy, advance the calendar, and see the next
  authored day beat.

## Self-directed play

Press `J` for the Valley Journal. It presents an optional personal request,
relationships, settlement identity, northern-ruin discoveries, the travelling
merchant, and Harvest Feast preparation. These systems are independent of the
linear campaign.

- `Space` or `1`: deliver the optional daily request.
- `2`: contribute bread to the Day 8 feast.
- `3`: buy the merchant's rotating decoration on his Day 6 visit.
- `Esc`: return to play.

Requests improve a named villager's bond and Community. The Day 8 text changes
according to feast preparation. The first relationship characters have
personal story lines that change as their bond grows.

## Discovery and customization

Wild herbs and a unique carved fragment are hidden in the north-east ruins.
They can be preserved in the Royal Cabinet. The fragment reveals a clue about
the traveler's forgotten connection to the valley.

The merchant rotates between a village lantern, bench, and flower bed. These
are optional placeable objects, can be positioned like other plans, and improve
the settlement's Beauty rather than campaign completion.

All Living Timeline state participates in ordinary manual saves and autosaves.
